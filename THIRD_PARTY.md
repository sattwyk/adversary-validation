# Provenance and licenses

Upstream source is acquired into ignored directories. Preserve each repository's native licenses there: raft-log and OpenRaft MIT/Apache-2.0, historical etcd Apache-2.0, SQLite public domain, historical HashiCorp Raft MPL-2.0, Pebble BSD-3-Clause. Verify license files at each revision when distributing any copied material.

Research prose/code is referenced by immutable links, not vendored or relicensed. adversary-research declares no project-wide license. Generated hashes and new specifications do not change that. Two small upstream regression tests are copied as test-only patches for A and B, with origin fix commits, patch hashes, unmodified source notices and original license texts in their patches directories. Those files retain their upstream licenses.
